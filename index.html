About me

The person
behind the pixels.

Natalia Cichocka

UX Designer · 7+ years

I'm a UX Designer with a background in Psychology and Art History — which means I think about people as much as I think about design.

For over seven years I've been working at the intersection of research and design: interviewing users, building design systems, running usability tests, and making sure software is accessible to everyone who needs it.

Right now I'm designing medical device software at a leading global life sciences company, working with multidisciplinary teams across Poland, Spain, and the UK to meet FDA and WCAG 2.2 standards. Before that I worked on university tools, library systems, and e-commerce for international brands across FMCG, consumer goods, and lifestyle retail.
