About me

The person
behind the pixels.

I'm a UX Designer with a background in Psychology and Art History — which means I think about people as much as pixels. For over seven years I've been working at the intersection of research and design: interviewing users, building design systems, running usability tests, and making sure software is accessible to everyone who needs it.

Right now I'm designing medical device software at Qiagen (via Sii), working with teams across Poland, Spain, and the UK to meet FDA standards and WCAG 2.2 requirements. Previously I've worked on university tools, library systems, and e-commerce for global brands through Gorilla Group and American Systems.
